How it works

Less searching. More certainty before you act.

Across all three products, the method is the same: filter aggressively, test what survives, and show the complete decision.

01

Filter the noise

Most opportunities never reach you. Weak evidence and poor conditions are rejected first.

02

The short list is checked against evidence, price, risk, costs, and real-world execution.

03

Make the decision clear

You see the opportunity, the risk, and what changes the call—or a clear stand-down.

How the research is verified
TSM combines historical and point-in-time data with quantitative, technical, risk, cost, and liquidity checks. Decisions are recorded before outcomes are known so the misses, passes, and stand-downs remain visible.
The problem

You don't need more alerts. You need fewer, better decisions.

The hard part of investing isn't finding something to look at — it's separating the few situations worth your capital from the endless stream that isn't.

The foundation

From thousands of inputs to a few complete decisions.

TSM runs on a mature research infrastructure. It tests methods across years of historical market data, combines that with point-in-time market inputs, quantitative analysis, technical analysis, and risk/cost/liquidity filters — then rejects most candidates to surface the few that deserve a complete plan. Or it says plainly: nothing today.

Options

See what is preparing before it becomes a trade.

The live Options board separates next-open candidates from executable plans. A stock is not assigned to Scalp or Swing until its timing, contract, entry, and risk all qualify.

Prepare before the open. See the direction, defined trade level, volume behavior, buyer or seller imbalance, rejection level, and broader-market alignment.

Wait for the contract. Nothing is presented as executable until the market is open and the option contract confirms.
