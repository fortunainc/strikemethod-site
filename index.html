How it works

One question, answered.

TSM is decision-support software, not a signal feed. It is being developed to do the analysis so you walk in with a shortlist instead of a blank chart.

01

Designed to filter the board

TSM is being developed to evaluate a broad options universe and narrow the field before the trading day begins.

02

Designed to surface only what clears the bar

The product standard is a complete, structured plan—or no trade. Never an unexplained alert or bare number.

03
