How it works

Filter. Test. Decide.

TSM is built to do the work that usually stands between you and a good decision — narrowing a huge market down to a few complete, checkable calls.

01

It filters out most of the market

Thousands of names and contracts are reviewed and most are set aside. What is easy to find is rarely worth your capital; TSM is built to discard the noise before you ever see it.

02

It tests what survives

The few candidates left are checked against realistic costs, liquidity, entry price, and the conditions that would make the idea wrong — not just whether the chart looks good.

03

It hands you a complete decision

You get the setup, the risk, and what would change it — or an honest "nothing today." Every call is recorded before the outcome is known, so the record is judged, not the marketing.

Options · priority product

Finding a moving stock is easy. Knowing which contract is worth buying is the hard part.

You can be right about a stock and still lose on options — because the contract, the entry price, the timing, the liquidity, or the exit was wrong. TSM is built to review a broad market, reject the weak and untradeable setups, and turn the strongest survivors into complete plans.

What you receive. Not a bare ticker or a one-line alert — the whole decision: the contract, why the setup matters, where to enter, where the idea is wrong, where you take profit, the time horizon, liquidity and cost checks, and how to exit.

And when it doesn't qualify, TSM says no trade — with the reason kept on the record instead of quietly moved past.

How this helps you

It shows what a stock actually leans on — and where two or more of your holdings quietly depend on the same supplier, technology, or rule. That is concentration you can't see from a ticker or a chart.

Every connection is meant to trace back to a source you can check. This is a preview of the map, not a claim that it predicts returns — we are building it to make hidden dependencies and risks visible, and testing whether those relationships hold up.

The foundation

Built on market history — not market hype.

TSM does not form decisions from social-media trends, hot takes, or the last few charts. It is built to test ideas against long stretches of market history, under realistic conditions, before asking you to trust them.

Point-in-time

Only what was known then

History is replayed in order, using the information that would actually have been available at the time — so a past decision can't be flattered by facts it couldn't have had.

Realistic

Costs, liquidity, and slippage

Ideas are tested with realistic assumptions about fees, spreads, and how an order would actually fill — not on frictionless paper prices.

Honest record

Rejections kept, not hidden

Losing and rejected candidates are kept as part of the evidence. Historical testing is not a promise of future results; the point is to find what survives and discard what doesn't.

A method only earns a place in the product if it holds up out-of-sample and improves the decision. If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.

Sports prediction markets

A focused research lane — probability against price.

A smaller, bounded research effort alongside the main products. The idea is simple: estimate the probability of an outcome, then ask whether the price on offer is actually worth taking.

Research · paper only

Would the difference survive the costs?

An outcome feeling likely is not a reason to bet. TSM compares its estimated probability with the market's price, then checks whether the difference is large enough to matter after fees, liquidity, sizing, and risk.

  • Compare probability with the price being offered
  • Reject apparent opportunities that disappear after costs
  • Limit repeated or correlated exposure

How TSM earns trust

Judge the process — not the pitch.

TSM is being built in closed beta while its Options and Long-Term Stocks products build a forward record. It is designed to be judged by how it works, not by a promise.

1

The decision is recorded first

Each call is written down before the outcome is known — so the record can be checked, not curated after the fact.

2

Misses are not deleted

Losing calls and rejected ideas stay in the record. A track record that only shows winners isn't one.

3

Results include the real costs

Fees, spreads, and realistic execution are part of the test — not stripped out to make a number look better.

4

A new method must beat the current one

Nothing replaces what's already there unless it does better under the same test. Cleverness alone isn't enough.

5

"No trade" is a real answer

TSM can stand down. When nothing clears the bar, saying so plainly is the product working — not failing.

6

History and live results stay separate

What was tested on the past and what is being proven going forward are never blended into one number.
