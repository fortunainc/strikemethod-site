# Lead and interview schema

Store PII only in the authorized form/CRM. The research register uses a pseudonymous lead ID.

## Lead fields

`lead_id`, `captured_at`, `consent_status`, `primary_interest`, `biggest_challenge`, `current_tool_spend`, `investment_context`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, `referrer`, `research_variant`, `qualification_state`, `invitation_state`, `product_access_state`.

## Interview fields

`interview_id`, `lead_id`, `lane`, `date`, `permission_to_record`, `active_behavior`, `last_decision_walkthrough`, `tools_checked`, `signals_in_conflict`, `missing_information`, `current_spend`, `canceled_tools`, `replacement_candidate`, `quiet_day_reaction`, `transparent_loss_reaction`, `price_tradeoff`, `commitment_level`, `limitations`, `evidence_status`.

Commitment levels: compliment, curiosity, problem confirmation, product intent, switching intent, payment intent, actual payment.
