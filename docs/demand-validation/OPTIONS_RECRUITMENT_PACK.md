## Channel sequence

1. Founder-owned audience where permission to invite research participation already exists.
2. Permission-based options communities that allow research or beta-recruitment posts.
3. Paid-tool switchers recruited through authorized customer-research platforms, only with a separately approved incentive/budget.
4. Paid acquisition only after an organic cell produces qualified completions and a budget is separately approved.

Never scrape or cold-message community members. Record source, placement, date, audience rule, incentive, impressions if available, clicks, starts, quick completions, qualified completions, and interview acceptances.

## Interpretation

- Do not name a message winner before at least 30 qualified Options respondents across comparable cells.
- Quick email submissions are leads, not qualified respondents.
- Primary comparison: qualified completions per attributable landing-page visit.
- Secondary: paid-tool incidence, interview acceptance, recent switching, and stated workflow replacement.
- A cell loses if it attracts mostly beginners, intraday traders, zero-spend curiosity, or performance-seeking language even when raw signup volume is high.
