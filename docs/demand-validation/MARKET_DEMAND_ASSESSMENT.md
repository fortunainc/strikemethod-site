# Market demand assessment

As of: 2026-09-01

## Answer

**Yes: sufficient market demand exists to justify launching a focused TSM Options offer into validation and early access.** The evidence does not justify launching all three lanes or treating a broad “AI trading platform” as the product.

The most commercially supported job is: help active self-directed Options traders turn abundant, conflicting inputs into a small number of complete swing-trade decisions, including explicit rejection/no-trade outcomes and an accountable record.

This conclusion is about market demand, not proof that the current TSM product has achieved product-market fit.

## Demand test

| Question | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Is the underlying market active and growing? | OCC reported 15.207 billion U.S. options contracts in 2025, +24.4% year over year; Cboe reported a sixth consecutive record year and 61 million contracts daily. | Yes |
| Is the target audience materially retail? | Cboe estimated retail represented roughly 50–60% of SPX 0DTE trading in 2025 and reported a retail rebound in Q2 2026. | Yes |
| Do customers already pay for adjacent solutions? | Current consumer products charge roughly $35–$99/month for journaling and $50+/month for options-flow tools. | Yes |
| Are there scaled paid substitutes? | TradeZella reports 100K+ traders and 20.2B journaled trades; Unusual Whales reports 50K+ active traders, while Google Play shows 50K+ Android installs. These are vendor/platform-reported and must not be summed as unique customers. | Yes |
| Is the pain aligned with TSM rather than generic trading desire? | Public samples repeatedly describe too many flow records, difficulty knowing what is actionable, missing hand-holding, manual journaling burden, rule-breaking, and execution-time doubt. | Yes, strongest for decision compression + complete plan; moderate for receipts/rejection |
| Is the proposed price plausible? | Existing consumer spend brackets overlap $49 and $89. $129 exceeds common journal tiers and requires stronger replacement value or bundled workflow. | $49 plausible; $89 plausible for active paid-tool users; $129 unproven |
| Is the audience reachable? | Large options communities, competitor app audiences, trading-journal communities, broker ecosystems, and creator/education channels exist. Channel economics and TSM conversion remain unmeasured. | Yes, with acquisition-risk caveat |

## Segment decision

| Segment | Market demand | TSM fit | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active self-directed swing-options traders already paying for tools | High enough for entry | Strong: information overload, contract/plan completeness, discipline and records | Launch first into controlled validation |
| Options beginners | Large but poor initial fit | High education/support burden; risk of treating TSM as recommendations | Defer |
| Intraday/0DTE Options | Large and growing | Product remains shadow; speed and execution expectations conflict with current truth | Defer commercially |
| Capital hidden-dependency research | Paid research market exists | Specific pain/purchase relevance not yet strong enough | Continue research, do not lead launch |
| Sports/prediction markets | Strong paid analytics category | Product paper/shadow state and entrenched price tools weaken immediate launch | Defer launch; validate separately |

## Minimum commercially viable experience

For the first Options cohort, the product must deliver:

1. A deliberately small, governed swing-options decision set.
2. Complete contract, entry, risk, target, invalidation, horizon, and exit logic.
3. Explicit rejected/no-trade states with understandable reasons.
4. Decision-time evidence and contradictions without exposing proprietary implementation.
5. Permanent prospective receipts, including losses and quiet periods.
6. A simple return workflow; no scanner, terminal, generic chatbot, or alert firehose.

Personal calibration (`Your Edge`) is not required for initial launch and should not command a premium until sufficient user history exists.

## Pricing and positioning

- Recommended test entry: **$49 introductory access**.
- Primary paid hypothesis: **$89 founding Core** for users replacing at least one research/journal/alert expense.
- Hold **$129 standard Core** as an upper hypothesis until real replacement and retention evidence exists.
- Positioning: **“A few complete options decisions—or an honest stand-down—with the plan and receipt attached.”**
- Do not lead with AI, flow, scanning, predictions, win rate, or performance.

## Evidence boundaries

This assessment demonstrates an addressable, paying problem market and a defensible initial wedge. It does not demonstrate TSM conversion, acquisition cost, retention, payment, economic success, or product-market fit. Competitor user counts are vendor-reported; public discussions are selected samples; trading volume is activity, not a customer count.
